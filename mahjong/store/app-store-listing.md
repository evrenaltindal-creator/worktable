## English (en-US)

**Name:** Huzur Mahjong

**Subtitle:** Calm tile matching, big tiles

**Promotional Text:**
Big, easy-to-read tiles and no timer pressure. Match pairs, clear the board, and relax.

**Keywords:**
mahjong,tile,match,puzzle,solitaire,brain,classic,big,large,easy,relaxing,offline,pairs

**Description:**
Huzur Mahjong brings the classic tile-matching game to your device with a calm,
easy-on-the-eyes design.

FIND MATCHING PAIRS
Tap two identical tiles that are free — nothing on top and an open left or right side —
to remove them. Clear every tile to finish the level.

EASY ON THE EYES
• Big, clear tiles that stay readable on small screens
• Playable tiles are bright, blocked tiles are dimmed
• Large buttons and large text

NO RUSH
• No timer pressure
• Unlimited hints, undo and shuffle
• Every level is guaranteed solvable

PLENTY TO PLAY
• 200 levels across 16 layouts: heart, house, butterfly, castle, pyramid and more
• Your game is saved automatically

NO INTERNET NEEDED
Huzur Mahjong works offline and collects no personal data.

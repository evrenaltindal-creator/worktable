Everyone taking part gets a turn before anyone repeats. Weights are ignored.

Each draw is independent. Weights set the odds; equal results are not guaranteed.

Picks equally among available people with the lowest pending + completed effort points this period.

Skipped: no points, but the turn is used.

Canceled: no points; the turn is returned within the same round.

Group names, participants, tasks, rounds and history stay on this device. No accounts, ads, analytics or automatic sync. You download backup files; sharing them sends their contents to your chosen recipient. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data may erase local records.

There is no automatic cloud backup. Download backups regularly to protect your groups.
